# AlphaLab 0.6.1 · dev_liu

This release continues development on `dev_liu` from the 0.6.0 dongfang/dev_liu integration commit `b8e8180`. SDK v1 remains the only strategy execution path.

- Adds technical-evidence calculations with dates, currency, price basis, volume units, source, and input fingerprints. Supported measures include moving averages, returns, MACD, RSI, KDJ, TR/ATR, Bollinger Bands, CCI, OBV, and explicit pattern conditions. Insufficient history, missing benchmarks, and unverified calendars are disclosed.
- Adds three SDK-native factor templates: lower-shadow recovery, three white soldiers, and volume-confirmed breakout. These are research filters; improved out-of-sample net returns have not been established.
- Adds an annual-return audit distinguishing compounded cumulative returns, CAGR over the actual number of years, and the proportion of years outperforming a benchmark.
- Provides installable technical-evidence and factor-replication audit skills. See the SDK guide for commands and input conventions.

The published table on page 14 of *Chapter 6: Multifactor and Barra Models 2026* was recalculated, and previously frozen local research artifacts were consolidated. The lecture's 2010–2020 sample spans 11 years, while its published annualized values match a 12-year convention. Recalculating a table is not an independent stock-level backtest. The earlier research did not align the universe, factor proxies, or portfolio rules with the lecture; its quality portfolio did not reproduce the reported advantage. Exact definitions and corresponding data remain missing for growth, dividend, and the lecture's original quality-plus-value index.

The SNOW example used 47 public daily bars through 2026-09-04. Longer moving averages cannot be verified from that window, and recursive indicators depend on initialization. The report does not infer investor intent from OBV or supply forecast probabilities without historical testing.

Research data, reports, market snapshots, and test logs are stored in ignored artifacts. The complete release bundle also included research attachments and a verification manifest. See [release notes](../README.md#release-notes) for later versions.
