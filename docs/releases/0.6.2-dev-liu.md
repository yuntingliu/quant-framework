# AlphaLab 0.6.2 · dev_liu

This release corrects the financial-data transformation used by quality factors and runs seven new stock-level comparisons through SDK v1 over the full 2019 and 2020 calendar years. It builds on 0.6.1 and retains the single execution path from the dongfang/dev_liu integration.

The previous 2020 return of 4.46% came from a CNY 200,000 account with 100-share lots, an average of 34.17 holdings, and 33.72% cash. It is not directly comparable to a theoretical quality index. The new monthly equal-weight portfolio of the top 50 historical CSI 300 constituents by average-equity ROE returned 44.43% in 2019 and 44.31% in 2020, versus 44.62% and 42.64% in the lecture. Applying the same rules to all A-shares produced 19.97% and 18.40%, illustrating the importance of the universe in this comparison.

| Change | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Financial visibility | Original disclosures become usable no earlier than the next day; adjusted-only quarters are not treated as original records |
| TTM and ROE | Match exact quarters and dependency disclosure dates; use average positive equity from the current and year-earlier periods |
| ROA and diagnostics | Add an average-total-assets proxy, closing-equity ROE, and traceable TTM profit fields |
| Quality template | Average the ranks of ROE, ROA, and low leverage; exclude missing or infinite inputs |
| Replication experiment | Freeze local caches, run seven fixed comparisons through canonical project-save APIs, and retain daily weights and fills |
| Large-sample performance | Pivot only required SDK fields and the effective trailing window while preserving sparse-history semantics |

This remains a transparent approximation. The lecture does not identify the quality-index code, exact sample, or rebalance method. Only 2019–2020 was rerun; the full 11-year period, out-of-sample benefit, and machine-learning gains were not established. Results use fractional weights with proportional commission and slippage, without a separate 100-share-lot, minimum-commission, or stamp-duty account ledger. Historical constituents are monthly snapshots, and saved strategy rules estimate missing price limits. The 44.31% result is neither an exact replication nor the realizable return of a CNY 200,000 account.

Commands and input conventions are in the [SDK guide](../06_ALPHALAB_SDK_GUIDE.md). Experiment tables, previous-account diagnostics, snapshot hashes, seven run outputs, and reports were provided as research attachments in the release bundle. Market caches and generated research artifacts are excluded from source control. See [release notes](../README.md#release-notes) for other versions.
